Game character design sheet for a cute medieval-fantasy strategy game, bright cheerful cartoon sticker style: thick dark outlines, flat colours, one hard cel shadow, chunky chibi proportions with big heads (about 2 heads tall), readable at very small size. Plain pure white background. Nine separate characters in three rows of three, each standing apart with plenty of white space between them, full body, three-quarter front view facing right, no overlap, no ground, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
All nine belong to the same army and wear the same royal blue team colour on their cloth (tunic, cape, hood, plume or banner); everything else in warm earthy colours (leather brown, steel grey, skin tones, wood). No other blue anywhere.
Row 1:
1. Militia: a scrappy peasant recruit with a pitchfork and a blue tunic, patched trousers, eager grin.
2. Slinger: a small nimble kid in a blue hood swinging a leather sling over his head, satchel of stones.
3. Shieldbearer: a stout soldier hidden behind a huge round wooden shield with a blue rim, short sword, kettle helmet.
Row 2:
4. Bowman: a lean archer with a long bow drawn, blue cape, feathered cap, quiver on the back.
5. Ogre Brute: a huge green-skinned ogre with a big belly, tusks and a spiked wooden club, a ragged blue sash, twice as wide as the others.
6. Storm Adept: a young wizard in a blue robe and pointy hat raising a staff crackling with bright yellow lightning.
Row 3:
7. Stone Colossus: a heavy golem of grey boulders with glowing yellow eyes, a blue banner cloth tied around its waist, much taller than the others.
8. Flame Drake: a small fat orange baby dragon with stubby wings, breathing a little flame, wearing a blue saddle blanket.
9. Castle Guard: a knight in shining steel armour with a blue plume and a blue tabard, holding a long spear and a kite shield.
